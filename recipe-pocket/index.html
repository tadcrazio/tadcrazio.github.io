A few things to know

Ready for your kitchen.

Where can I download Recipe Pocket?

Recipe Pocket is preparing for its first App Store and Google Play releases. Download links will appear here when the app is available. It is not published in either store yet.

Is Recipe Pocket free?

The planned download is free, with optional Buy Me a Coffee support. AI features use your own provider API key. Provider charges, free-tier eligibility and usage limits are separate from the app.

Which features need an AI key?

Creating, editing and reading your saved cookbook, supported text/website parsing, cook mode and backups do not need AI. Written-recipe photo scanning, AI-assisted imports and nutrition estimates use a provider key. YouTube understanding requires Gemini. Other supported AI providers include OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI.

Will my recipes disappear if a website or video goes away?

Once imported and saved, recipe text and cached photos remain in your local cookbook for offline use. Export a backup to protect against losing your device or clearing app storage. Internet access is needed for new imports and online features.

Can I print or change a recipe?

Yes. Edit your saved recipe, write your own, and use print/PDF to make a copy you can share. Nutrition can be imported when available or optionally estimated with AI when missing.

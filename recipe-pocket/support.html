A helping hand

Recipe Pocket support

Need help with your cookbook? Get in touch and we’ll help you find your way.

Email support
recipepocket@duck.com

Include your device model, Android or iOS version, the recipe source link if relevant, and the error you see. Please never send API keys, passwords or payment details.

Importing a recipe

Open Add recipe and paste a recipe link or the full ingredients and directions. Some websites block automated access or require a subscription. When a link cannot be read, paste the recipe text you can access. You can also create a recipe manually.

Optional AI features

Ordinary text imports and your saved cookbook do not need an AI key. AI-assisted imports, written-recipe photo scanning and nutrition estimates use a key you add in Settings. The app supports Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI. A chat subscription does not automatically include API access; provider availability, usage limits and charges apply separately.

YouTube understanding uses Google Gemini. A Gemini key is required for this feature; Google determines free-tier eligibility and limits. Review the sharing prompt before sending recipe text, a photo or a video link to a provider.

Back up your cookbook

Use Export backup in My cookbook and save the file somewhere safe. Use Restore backup to bring it back. Backups include recipes and cached photos, but exclude API keys. Saved recipes can be read offline; new imports and online AI features need a connection.

Edit, print and share

Open a saved recipe to edit its ingredients, directions and details. The print/PDF action creates a copy you can save or share using your device’s available destinations.

Feedback

Suggest a feature or report an issue using the feedback section. It opens a draft in your own email app so you can review and send it.

Privacy and support

Read the privacy policy for details about local storage and optional third-party services. Recipe Pocket is planned as a free download.

The optional Buy Me a Coffee page supports development and does not unlock app features.

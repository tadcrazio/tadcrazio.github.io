Privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026.

Developer: Anthony Ciccone / Recipe Pocket

Privacy and support contact: recipepocket@duck.com

Recipe Pocket stores your saved recipes, cookbook organization, settings, cooking progress and cached photos on your device. The Android app encrypts your optional AI API keys with Android Keystore. The Apple version uses Apple Keychain, with keys restricted to the device. Recipe Pocket does not operate a user account system or a cookbook synchronization server in this version. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

Cooking timers, shopping lists, meal plans and prep checklists are saved on your device. Timer names and deadlines are shared with your device notification system; they may appear on the lock screen. Android retains timer names and deadlines in device-protected storage so alarms can recover before the first unlock after a restart. This storage does not contain provider API keys. Where supported, Apple Live Activities display active timers.

Recipe changes and photos have dated recovery copies on your device. Recipes moved to Recently deleted and older versions remain in recovery history and complete backups; moving a recipe there does not immediately erase those copies. Local recovery copies do not protect against losing the device or uninstalling the app. Complete backup exports include recipes, cached photos, versions, meal plans and prep tasks; provider API keys are excluded. Optional folder sync writes recipes, photos and version history to a folder you choose through the system file picker. Your selected drive provider may upload those files under its own terms and privacy practices. Recipe Pocket has no cookbook sync server. Meal plans, shopping lists and timers are not synchronized between devices in this version.

Voice navigation is optional, requests microphone and speech-recognition access, and requires supported on-device speech recognition. Audio is processed by the device's on-device recognition service; Recipe Pocket does not store recordings or send them to an AI provider. The microphone stops when you leave cooking or background the app. An installed language model may be required.

When you import a website, the app contacts that website to retrieve its content. When you choose to discover online recipes, your ingredient queries are sent to TheMealDB; recipe details and images are retrieved from that service and relevant image sources. These services receive normal connection information, including your IP address. Content providers may apply their own privacy practices.

If you enable an AI provider and allow recipe sharing, the app sends the recipe text or link needed for the requested AI feature to that provider. Photo scanning sends the selected recipe, fridge or grocery photo. AI recipe suggestions send your confirmed ingredient list. Nutrition estimation sends ingredient quantities and the portion count. YouTube understanding sends the supplied video link to Google Gemini. Your API key is sent to its corresponding provider to authenticate those requests. The provider can associate these requests with your provider account. Depending on the provider, account tier and settings, inputs may be retained and used for service or model improvement. Review your provider’s privacy and data controls before using AI features. You select the provider, and their terms, retention practices and charges apply. Providers may include Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI. Recipe Pocket cannot delete copies retained independently by those providers; use their account controls and privacy contacts.

Illustrative dish-photo search sends the dish name to Pixabay, Openverse, Wikimedia Commons and WordPress Photo Directory and retrieves matching photos from the selected photo service and image servers. You can disable automatic photo search in Settings or when importing. These services receive normal connection information, including your IP address. Selected illustrative photos and their creator, source and license credits are saved locally and included when sharing, printing or exporting the recipe.

Source recipe images may be downloaded and stored locally. Selected or scanned cover images are resized and re-encoded; native temporary camera files are deleted after processing or cancellation. System camera, photo and file pickers provide the media you choose; the app does not request access to your entire photo library. Exporting, printing, or sharing a recipe or backup passes the selected content to the destination you choose. A recipe backup includes recipe text and cached photos, but not API keys. Keep exported files somewhere you trust.

You can delete individual recipes and remove provider keys in Settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes its local cookbook data. On Apple devices, remove provider keys in Settings before uninstalling; Keychain storage is managed by iOS, and uninstalling is not a substitute for removing those keys. Remove exported copies separately from the locations where you saved or shared them. Deleting a saved recipe does not delete its original website or a provider's separately retained data.

Read-aloud uses your device’s speech service. This feature does not record your voice or send recipe text to an AI provider. An installed voice is required.

Recipe Pocket provides general recipe information. Nutrition estimates are not medical advice. The developer will update this policy when the app's data practices change.

Where available under storefront rules, the optional Buy Me a Coffee footer link opens an external support page in your browser. Opening it is your choice; Buy Me a Coffee and its payment providers process any information or payment you choose to provide there under their own terms. Recipe Pocket does not embed its payment widget or handle your payment details.

This support website does not use advertising or analytics scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes normal connection information, including IP addresses and request logs, to serve and protect the site. The feedback form only opens an email draft on your device; it does not send or store submissions on this website. If you email support, the developer and email service process your address and the information you send to respond to your request. Avoid sending secrets or sensitive personal information.
